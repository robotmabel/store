Shipping, returns and the rest.

Shipping

Where do you ship from, and to?

Everything ships from Toronto, Canada. We ship to Canada and the United States by courier as standard, and worldwide on request. Complete robots ship crated by freight.

What does shipping cost?

Flat US$24 to Canada and the continental United States, free over US$500. Express is US$48. Freight for robots and heavy orders is quoted after you order, once we know the destination — we would rather book it properly than guess at checkout.

How long does it take?

Stocked parts leave within one to two business days. The MABEL Developer Kit ships in three to four weeks. An assembled MABEL has an eight to ten week lead time.

Duties and import taxes?

Outside Canada, duties and import taxes are the buyer's responsibility and are not collected at checkout. We declare goods accurately; we will not under-declare a shipment.

Condition and stock

What exactly am I buying?

Surplus from our own MABEL build. These are parts we ordered for the robot: spares held against a failure, the remainder of a minimum order quantity, or an option we evaluated and did not keep. Unless a listing says otherwise they are unused and in their original packaging — not used, not pulled, not refurbished. Where a part was fitted and removed, the listing says so.

How many of each do you have?

One build's worth. Quantities are small and finite, and a line that sells out may not come back — we are not a distributor with a re-supply pipeline. If you need production quantities, tell us and we will quote them new, on the normal lead time.

Is the warranty different on surplus parts?

Yes. Surplus parts carry a ninety-day functional warranty: if it does not work, we replace it or refund you. Complete MABEL robots and sub-assemblies we build are covered for two years, because we made those. Wear parts — tendons, fingertip pads, belts, tyres — are sold as consumables.

Are the photographs of the actual item?

Where the caption says so, yes — those are photographs of our own build. Where it credits a manufacturer, it is their product photograph of the same model. Parts we have not photographed yet show a generated illustration, and the caption says that too. We would rather tell you which is which than let you assume.

Returns

What is your returns policy?

Thirty days from delivery on unopened, unused parts in their original packaging, for a full refund less return shipping. Opened electronics and actuators can be returned within thirty days if unused and undamaged, subject to a 15% restocking fee.

What cannot be returned?

Cut-to-length extrusion, custom-machined parts, opened silicone and filament, and made-to-order robots and sub-assemblies. If a made-to-order item is faulty, that is a warranty matter and we will make it right.

Something arrived damaged.

Photograph it before you unpack any further and email us the same day. We will ship a replacement immediately and deal with the carrier ourselves.

Warranty

What is covered?

Ninety days on surplus parts, functional: if it does not work, we replace it or refund you. Two years on complete MABEL robots and on sub-assemblies we build, against defects in materials and workmanship. Wear parts — tendons, fingertip pads, belts, tyres — are consumables and are not covered.

What voids it?

Nothing you do in software, and nothing you do mechanically that a build guide told you to do. Modifying a robot does not void its warranty. Driving a joint past its documented torque limit, or running an actuator off a supply outside its rated voltage, does.

Bulk, education and purchase orders

Do you sell to universities on a purchase order?

Yes. Select “Invoice / purchase order” at checkout and enter your PO number, or email us the requisition and we will quote formally. Net 30 on approval.

Is there education pricing?

Yes, for degree-granting institutions and for teaching labs buying more than one robot. Email us from your institutional address with what you are building.

Can you quote a custom kit?

Yes. Send us your bill of materials, or a description of the robot, and we will price it and tell you honestly which lines you would be better off buying elsewhere.

Privacy

What do you store?

This site keeps your bag, your currency preference, your saved delivery details and your order references in your own browser's local storage. They are not sent to us until you place an order. There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking on this site.

What happens when I order?

Your name, email, address and order contents go to our fulfilment system so we can ship to you, and to the payment processor so it can take payment. Card details are handled entirely by the processor and never reach this site.

Accessibility

How accessible is this site?

We target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every control is reachable and operable by keyboard, focus is always visible, the bag and menu trap focus while open and restore it on close, images carry alternative text, colour contrast meets AA at every size, and the site respects prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme. If something here does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it — that is a bug, not a feature request.
