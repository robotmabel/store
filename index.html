Why buy from us

We ship the parts list of a robot that works.

Sourcing a research robot means forty tabs open across four continents, a spreadsheet of exchange rates and a six-week wait to find out the module you ordered does not include its motor. We have already done that. Twice.

Nothing is a mystery lump
