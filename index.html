Where the parts come from

These are our spares.

Every part in this catalogue was bought to build MABEL. Each one went into the robot, or sat on the shelf beside it as a spare.

What is listed here is the surplus: spares we held against a failure that never came, the remainder of minimum order quantities that do not divide by the number of joints on a robot, and the options we tried and did not keep. It is unused and in its original packaging — and there is one build's worth of it, so when a line sells out it may be gone for good.

That is also why we can tell you what each part is actually like. We did not read the datasheet. We fitted it.

Why buy from us

We ship the parts list of a robot that works.

Sourcing a research robot means forty tabs open across four continents, a spreadsheet of exchange rates and a six-week wait to find out the module you ordered does not include its motor. We have already done that. Twice.

Nothing is a mystery lump

A swerve module here ships with its motors, controllers and framing. We list what is in the box because we got caught out by a catalogue that did not.

Priced from a real bill of materials
